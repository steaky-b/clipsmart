FAQ

01
What makes ClipSmart different to a normal UGC agency?
You pay for results, not effort. Hundreds of creators post simultaneously, and if the agreed view target isn't hit, you get a full refund.
02
How are creators vetted and content reviewed?
Every creator is screened before joining our network, and every piece of content is manually reviewed by our team before it's allowed to go live.
03
Will this work for my niche — music, e-commerce, or podcasts?
Yes — the model is built around those three categories specifically, and each campaign is briefed around what actually works for your niche.
04
What happens if we don't hit the agreed view target?
You get a full refund. No dispute process, no small print — we either deliver the views or we don't charge for them.
